## 🎯 The 5 Core Fintech CRM Case Studies

### 1. 🚀 KYC & VideoIdent Drop-Off Recovery: The 'Locked Wallet' & Cross-Device Mechanism
* **The Challenge & Fintech Reality:** In Regulated Digital Asset Platform, new signups can explore live market data in a 'Restricted Read-Only Mode', but trading and deposits are locked behind BaFin VideoIdent verification. 41.8% abandon due to document friction or camera issues.
* **The Solution:** Automated behavioral Braze Canvas journey:
  - *Hour 2 (Push Notification):* Reassurance prompt (*'You are 2 minutes away from your wallet'* + deep link to VideoIdent SDK).
  - *Day 1 (Email):* Security trust guide highlighting Tier-1 European banking security and regulated exchange custody.
  - *Day 3 (In-App Banner):* €15 welcome bonus credit upon first SEPA bank transfer.
* **Business Impact:** **+28.4% KYC completion rate lift**.

### 2. 📈 'Sparplan' (DCA Recurring Savings) Adoption Engine
* **The Challenge:** Spot crypto traders experience high churn during bear/sideways markets. Recurring automated savings plans (Dollar Cost Averaging) produce **3.8x higher Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**.
* **The Solution:** Automated trigger 48h post-1st trade nudging users to enable recurring weekly/monthly crypto purchases with zero extra fees.
* **Business Impact:** **38.2% Sparplan adoption rate** and **-70.8% reduction in 90-day churn**.

### 3. ⚡ Volatility Alerts & Smart Deliverability Frequency Guard
* **The Challenge:** Crypto market swings ($\pm 5-10\%$) drive high trading volume, but uncoordinated alerts cause push notification opt-outs.
* **The Solution:** Portfolio-relevance filtering (only alert for coins the user holds/watches) + strict **24h cooling rules (max 2 pushes/day)**.
* **Business Impact:** **+192% trading reactivation velocity** while keeping opt-outs **< 0.15%**.

### 4. 👥 Crypto Trader RFM Clustering (Whales vs. HODLers)
* **The Challenge:** High-volume traders (Whales >€25k vol) require dedicated VIP execution, while passive HODLers need educational content.
* **The Solution:** Python RFM model clustering users by **Recency of Trade**, **Monthly Trade Frequency**, and **Total EUR Volume**.

### 5. 🔗 Braze Liquid Templating & Connected Content Live Engine
* **The Solution:** Live Liquid scripting pulling real-time crypto prices & portfolio balances securely via API into dynamic email and push payloads.
